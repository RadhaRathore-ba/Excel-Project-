💡 Project Overview:
- Designed an interactive dashboard to monitor Revenue, Base Price, Discount Price, and Final Price.
- Analyzed sales performance by Color, Category, Brand, Gender, Country, Month, and Year.
- Integrated multiple views and dynamic slicers to uncover trends and support data-driven decision-making.
- Created a clean and user-friendly layout for easy interpretation and reporting.

🚀 Business Problem Solved:
- Simplified the process of tracking footwear sales performance across multiple dimensions.
- Identified top-performing months, countries, and product categories to support better business decisions.
- Enabled dynamic analysis through filters for Size, Sales Channel, and Payment Method.
- Reduced manual reporting efforts by automating KPIs and visualizations in Excel.

⚙️ Excel Features Used:
📈 Pivot Tables | 🔍 VLOOKUP & INDEX-MATCH | ➕ SUMIFS, COUNTIFS | 📊 Charts (Column, Bar, Pie, Line) | 🎚️ Slicers & Filters | 🎨 Formatting & Dashboard Design

🧠 Key Insights:
- Total Revenue generated reached 186K.
- Sales showed a steady upward trend, peaking at 10K in 2026.
- July recorded the highest units sold.
- UAE, USA, and UK emerged as top revenue-contributing countries.
- Brand-wise and gender-wise analysis highlighted varying customer preferences.

- Strengthened my ability to convert raw data into actionable insights using Excel's analytical and visualization tools.
